## Severity-ranked findings and disposition

1. **Medium, resolved — macOS Idle could preserve an obsolete cached image.** The QA reviewer identified that a one-slot drop queue could reject a changed Complete image while Idle occupied the slot; an additional drain could also replace a received image with Idle. The wrapper's default Idle status for absent metadata compounded this risk. I independently inspected the replacement [mailbox](../../vendor/scap/src/capturer/engine/mac_mailbox.rs), [image-state policy](../../vendor/scap/src/capturer/engine/mac_frame_state.rs), raw status extraction and capture integration, then independently reran their portable regressions. Images replace older samples; Idle cannot overwrite a pending image or invalidation. Mac retrieval no longer drains a later sample over a selected image. Missing/unknown metadata, failed image conversion and backend errors invalidate Idle reuse. Sample release happens outside the mailbox mutex; deadlines and sender/receiver closure are bounded and tested. These fixes are coherent in source and portable tests, while their actual macOS framework behavior still needs native verification.

2. **Medium, resolved — bulb zones cloned and averaged identical regions.** My reviewer-created probe used a valid 64-bulb/2,048-zone/radius-1.0 layout. Before the fix it retained roughly 453 MiB RSS and averaged in 30.06 ms/frame. [SamplingPlan](../../src/core.rs) now keeps a single `Uniform` region per bulb and broadcasts one average to its configured zones; strips retain ordered regions. Independent final probe results were approximately 17.7 MiB RSS and 1.04 ms/frame, with light/zone cardinality and equal bulb colors checked. The new real-color regression also preserves interleaved light order, strip order and mismatch-to-black behavior. [Probe source](../evidence/continuous-architecture-probe.rs) and [pre/post results](../evidence/stress-continuous.log) are preserved. These are three-iteration synthetic sampling measurements, excluding native capture, smoothing, editor and networking; they are not general performance guarantees.

3. **Medium evidence limitation — current native platform behavior is not established.** Linux compilation does not compile the new macOS framework calls or callback integration. The 13 new portable vendor tests check the actual mailbox/status/image-state helpers, including priority, failed conversion, malformed statuses, wakeups, disconnection and sample drops; they do not invoke CoreMedia, ScreenCaptureKit or CoreVideo. Historical native CI for `ae6c0f1` does not cover these local changes. Current native UI, DPI/accessibility, real capture/permission flows, physical WLED/HA and OS keyring behavior were deliberately excluded. Preserve these distinctions; later authorized native validation should target those actual boundaries. This is a verification gap, not an inferred platform failure.

4. **Low, documented — maximum broad strip layouts remain expensive.** Ordered strip zones keep separate weight vectors and averages. Supported record/zone caps bound input size but do not promise a small sampling plan or a requested frame rate for every valid geometry. The corrected bulb probe does not establish strip worst-case costs. [Known issues](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md) now documents this limit and practical extent/zone/rate adjustments. A future measured weight budget or compact weight representation would improve predictability if large broad layouts become a requirement; no further optimization is required for this initial scope.

5. **Low, documented — the capability trait is not the dispatch abstraction.** `OutputAdapter`/`OutputCapabilities` in [outputs.rs](../../src/outputs.rs) have no concrete implementation or engine dispatch use. The working extension boundary is task ownership plus latest colors, Stop and status channels. Keeping this small capability sketch is reasonable for a future Hue Entertainment adapter, but it should not be described as an already integrated polymorphic framework. Dense WLED/HA loop expressions would benefit from small named helpers when those paths next change; a framework rewrite is unnecessary now.

## Architecture and initial-scope assessment

The application implements the initial scope coherently: scap-backed single display/window selection, a native egui 2D bulb/strip editor, normalized geometry and ordered sampling, WLED JSON inspection/control with DDP colors, optional HA WebSocket ambient control, credential-free persistent layouts, explicit Start/Pause/Stop/shutdown and synthetic/loopback diagnostics. Windows/macOS support exists in source; current runtime support is not proven. Hue Entertainment, HDR, multiple-source composition and daemon/tray behavior remain deferred rather than silently represented as implemented.

`core` validates geometry before compiling weights, averages in linear light and preserves zone order. `editor` isolates pure placement/history operations; snapshots have count and approximate combined byte bounds. `app` separates panels, discovery choice and layout interchange. Imported layouts validate before replacement and enter Undo as one edit. Tokens remain outside configuration/history/export. HA selection is explicit, searchable and virtualized; capability/availability filters and capacity bounds prevent indiscriminate discovery imports. WLED reinspection preserves a verified mapping and rejects a shrunken controller.

`engine` owns sessions and routing. Capture/normalization/sampling run on a dedicated worker, while immutable frame/configuration snapshots cross latest-value channels. Session identity rejects retired frames; safe live edits preserve route ownership and scalar smoothing. Capture cleanup runs on errors/unwind, low-FPS pacing can wake for Stop, and shutdown bounds queueing and network cleanup. A pending synchronous portal chooser remains noncancelable and blocks another worker, as documented. Explicit Idle refreshes a cached image only under the corrected backend policy; ordinary silence does not renew capture progress or publish cached colors.

WLED tasks group canonical physical identity, verify the saved controller before acquisition, check finite timeout/physical-index prerequisites, and release on stale capture or cancellation. Health helpers are canceled with their owners, including unwind/abort; IPv6 literals bypass bracketed hostname lookup. Retry backoff resets after actual local streaming recovery. UDP send success remains unconfirmed delivery. Previous-state restoration retains its original pre-acquisition baseline and remains non-atomic. HA has its own task, bounded acknowledgements, globally conservative pacing, round-robin selection and unchanged-color coalescing. Native credential reads/saves share one exclusion gate whose lease survives async timeout/cancellation until the blocking OS call returns.

These boundaries support the requested scope without a daemon, frontend service or unnecessary adapter framework. Failure/status behavior and remaining limits are explained in the current README, configuration, verification and known-issues documents. Historical review reports are preserved as evidence for earlier revisions, not descriptions of this final source.

Those permission failures are environment restrictions, not hidden product regressions. The separately reported temporary vendor style-lint failure was corrected before my final standalone vendor rerun.

Confidence is high in the inspected pure/editor/engine logic and mock adapter lifecycle, moderate in the overall desktop implementation, and limited for the deliberately untested native framework, compositor, credentials and physical-device boundaries.
